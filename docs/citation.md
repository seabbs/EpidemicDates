## Related work

- [BDBV-Modeling-Hub issue #61](https://github.com/InsightNet-US/BDBV-Modeling-Hub/issues/61) describes the imputation approach studied here.
- The presets use Ebola settings from [epiforecasts/BVDOutbreakSize](https://github.com/epiforecasts/BVDOutbreakSize) and [epiforecasts/bdbv-linelist-analysis](https://github.com/epiforecasts/bdbv-linelist-analysis).
- [epinowcast](https://package.epinowcast.org) models missing reference dates as part of the observation process.
- [primarycensored](https://primarycensored.epinowcast.org) and [epidist](https://epidist.epinowcast.org) estimate delays with censoring and truncation.
